# Application Decision Check

Run this before recommending, preparing, or submitting any job application.

## Role Fit

- Role title and responsibilities align with Javon's cyber, risk, regulatory, banking, compliance, audit remediation, AI governance, or financial services transformation background.
- Seniority is appropriate for manager, senior manager, lead, advisory, or comparable roles.
- Required qualifications are strongly or partially evidenced in the master profile.
- Preferred qualifications create upside but are not misrepresented as direct experience.

## Practical Fit

- Location, remote/hybrid expectations, travel, and schedule appear workable.
- Compensation is not clearly below target if compensation data is visible.
- Sponsorship, clearance, degree, or certification requirements do not create an unsupported claim.
- The application does not require references, legal attestations, assessments, or sensitive demographic responses without Javon's input.

## Conflict Check

Ask Javon before applying if there is any:

- Current employer, prior employer, client, vendor, or independence concern
- Confidential regulatory matter or client name issue
- Non-compete, independence, or reputational sensitivity
- Role at a direct competitor or firm connected to a current engagement

## Submission Check

- Resume is tailored and passes ATS quality gate.
- Cover letter or application answer is tailored when requested or required.
- No fabricated certifications, metrics, tools, client names, or degrees.
- No confidential information is disclosed.
- Javon approved the specific application unless he has explicitly enabled a narrow approved auto-submit mode.

## Decision Labels

Use one:

- `Apply - approved`
- `Recommend apply - awaiting Javon's approval`
- `Prepare only - needs missing answer`
- `Ask Javon - conflict or issue`
- `Watchlist`
- `Skip`

## Final Result Format

```markdown
Application Decision Gate:
- Role fit: Pass / Needs review - [reason]
- Practical fit: Pass / Needs review - [reason]
- Conflict check: Pass / Ask Javon - [reason]
- Submission readiness: Pass / Needs revision - [reason]
- Decision: [label]
```
