# Grill Me Intake

Ask only questions that materially affect accuracy, credibility, or confidentiality.

## Critical Questions

Use these when relevant:

1. Is this role targeting a one-page or two-page resume?
2. Are there any client names, program names, or regulatory matters you are comfortable naming for this application?
3. Does the role require a certification, clearance, degree, tool, or platform that is not already in your profile?
4. Do you have metrics for the most relevant work, such as number of issues remediated, controls tested, stakeholders supported, or team size?
5. Are you open to relocation, hybrid work, travel, or a specific salary range if the posting asks for it?
6. Do you want the resume to lean more toward cyber governance, regulatory remediation, enterprise risk, AI governance, or financial services transformation?
7. Should the cover letter mention why this company or industry is personally interesting to you?

## When Not To Ask

Do not ask questions just to make the output more polished. Proceed when:

- The job description is complete enough to map requirements.
- Missing metrics can be omitted instead of invented.
- Confidential details can be anonymized.
- The role direction is clear from the posting.

## Critical Question Format

```markdown
I can draft this now, but these answers would materially improve accuracy:

1. [Question]
2. [Question]

If you do not have these handy, I will proceed with conservative assumptions and avoid unsupported claims.
```
