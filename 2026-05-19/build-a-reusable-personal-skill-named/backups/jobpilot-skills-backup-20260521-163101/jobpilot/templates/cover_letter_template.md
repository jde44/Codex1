# Cover Letter Template

```markdown
Dear [Hiring Manager/Recruiting Team],

I am interested in the [Role Title] position at [Company]. My background sits at the intersection of cyber, risk, regulatory remediation, and financial services transformation, with 10+ years across advisory, banking, compliance, controls, and audit remediation environments.

In my current role as a Cyber, Risk & Regulatory Advisory Manager at PwC, I support complex risk and regulatory work for financial services clients, including issue management, root cause analysis, audit readiness, governance, and remediation execution. The parts of this role that stand out to me are [2-3 job-specific themes], which align closely with my experience helping stakeholders translate regulatory expectations into practical operating model, control, and governance improvements.

I also bring a practical interest in AI-enabled regulatory mapping, traceability, workflow automation, and governance. My recent professional development in AI, cloud, enterprise architecture, and responsible AI supports my ability to work with business, risk, compliance, cyber, and technology teams without overstating technical ownership.

I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my risk advisory, regulatory remediation, and stakeholder leadership experience can support [Company]'s priorities.

Best,
Javon Edwards
```

## Tone Rules

- Keep it specific to the company and role.
- Avoid flattery that sounds generic.
- Do not repeat the resume bullet-for-bullet.
- Do not invent personal motivation or company knowledge.
- Keep to 250-400 words unless the user asks otherwise.
