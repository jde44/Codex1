# Google ADK Marketing Agency

Use this skill when Javon asks to build or use a marketing-agency style ADK workflow for campaigns, brand messaging, content, or launch assets.

Source basis:
- Upstream: https://github.com/google/adk-samples
- Sample path: `python/agents/marketing-agency`
- License: Apache-2.0

Workflow:
1. Clarify audience, offer, brand voice, channel mix, budget, timeline, and success metrics.
2. Break work into strategy, positioning, content, creative direction, channel plan, and measurement.
3. Produce practical campaign assets with versioned options and rationale.
4. Keep claims grounded in provided brand facts, product proof, and market evidence.
5. When implementing, preserve modular ADK agents for strategy, copy, creative, and review.

Guardrails:
- Do not fabricate customer results, testimonials, awards, compliance claims, or product capabilities.
- Avoid spammy outreach, deceptive scarcity, and unsupported performance guarantees.
- Ask before using regulated industry claims or competitor comparisons.
