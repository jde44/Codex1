# Google ADK Academic Research

Use this skill when Javon asks to build or use an academic research agent for papers, literature review, citation analysis, or research summaries.

Source basis:
- Upstream: https://github.com/google/adk-samples
- Sample path: `python/agents/academic-research`
- License: Apache-2.0

Workflow:
1. Clarify the research question, field, date range, inclusion criteria, and citation format.
2. Prioritize peer-reviewed papers, preprints when appropriate, datasets, and primary sources.
3. Summarize methods, findings, limitations, disagreements, and research gaps.
4. Produce cited synthesis, annotated bibliographies, comparison tables, or study extraction grids.
5. When implementing, include source metadata, deduplication, and reproducible search terms.

Guardrails:
- Do not fabricate papers, authors, DOIs, findings, citations, or quotes.
- Distinguish peer-reviewed evidence from preprints, commentary, and speculation.
- Respect copyright limits when quoting or summarizing papers.
