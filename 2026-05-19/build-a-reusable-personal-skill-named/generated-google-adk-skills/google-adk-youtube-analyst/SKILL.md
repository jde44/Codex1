# Google ADK YouTube Analyst

Use this skill when Javon asks for the screenshot's YouBuddy-style functionality or any YouTube content analytics agent.

Source basis:
- Upstream: https://github.com/google/adk-samples
- Sample path: `python/agents/youtube-analyst`
- Screenshot mapping: the visible "YouBuddy" sample maps to this YouTube analyst source.
- License: Apache-2.0

Workflow:
1. Clarify the channel, video set, audience segment, dates, and success metric.
2. Gather available metadata, transcripts, comments, engagement metrics, and topic signals.
3. Analyze performance, themes, retention hypotheses, audience fit, and content gaps.
4. Recommend practical title, thumbnail, structure, publishing, and content-series improvements.
5. Provide source-backed observations and separate platform data from creative judgment.

Guardrails:
- Do not claim access to private YouTube analytics unless credentials/data are provided.
- Do not fabricate views, revenue, demographics, retention, or monetization status.
- Respect copyright, platform policies, and user privacy.
