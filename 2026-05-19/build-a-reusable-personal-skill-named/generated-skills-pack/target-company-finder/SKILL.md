# Target Company Finder

Create evidence-based target company lists for Javon's job search.

## Inputs

- Target role titles or functions
- Preferred industries
- Location/remote/hybrid preferences
- Company size/stage preference
- Exclusions or conflicts
- Desired themes: risk advisory, regulatory remediation, cyber governance, AI governance, compliance, fintech, consulting, transformation

## Process

1. Clarify search thesis only if missing.
2. Group companies by category:
   - Financial institutions
   - Fintech/regtech
   - Consulting/advisory
   - AI governance/risk platforms
   - Cyber/GRC platforms
   - Select healthcare or mortgage/lending targets if relevant
3. Score companies for:
   - Domain fit
   - Role likelihood
   - Network/referral potential
   - Location/remote fit
   - Conflict sensitivity
4. Flag companies requiring conflict or client-confidentiality review.

## Output

```markdown
## Target Company List

| Priority | Company | Category | Why It Fits | Target Roles | Outreach Angle | Conflict Check |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Top 10 To Work First

1. [Company] - [reason]

## Search Queries

- [Boolean/search query]
```

## Quality Rules

- Do not claim relationships Javon has not confirmed.
- Do not use confidential client names.
- Prefer practical, recruiter-searchable target titles.
- Include search terms Javon can use on LinkedIn, company sites, and trusted boards.
