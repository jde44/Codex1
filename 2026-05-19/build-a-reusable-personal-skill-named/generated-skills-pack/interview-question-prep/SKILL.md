# Interview Question Prep

Prepare interview content that is practical, specific, and evidence-grounded.

## Inputs

- Job description or target role
- Interview round type
- Company or industry
- Resume/application package if available

## Process

1. Identify likely interview themes.
2. Create role-specific questions.
3. Draft answer frameworks, not canned scripts.
4. Suggest STAR stories from known evidence.
5. Flag gaps and likely challenge questions.

## Output

```markdown
## Interview Prep

### Likely Themes
- [Theme]

### Questions and Answer Frameworks
| Question | What They Are Testing | Answer Framework | Evidence To Use |
|---|---|---|---|

### Questions Javon Should Ask
- [Question]

### Gaps To Address Directly
- [Gap and framing]
```
