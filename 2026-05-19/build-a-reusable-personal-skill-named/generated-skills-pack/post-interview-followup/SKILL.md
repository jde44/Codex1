# Post-Interview Follow-Up

Write concise follow-up messages after interviews.

## Inputs

- Interviewer name/title
- Role/company
- Topics discussed
- Concerns or gaps raised
- Desired next step

## Rules

- Keep it specific and brief.
- Reference real discussion points only.
- Do not over-apologize for gaps.
- Reinforce fit with evidence.

## Output

```markdown
## Thank-You Follow-Up

Subject: Thank you

Hi [Name],

[Message]

Best,
Javon

## If There Was A Concern
[Concern-addressing variant]

## Follow-Up After No Response
[Short nudge]
```
