# Hiring Manager Cold Outreach

Create concise hiring manager outreach that sounds human and credible.

## Inputs

- Hiring manager name/title if available
- Company and role
- Why the role/company fits
- One or two evidence-backed strengths
- Desired call to action

## Rules

- Keep under 120 words unless asked otherwise.
- Be specific, not flattering.
- Do not imply a referral, connection, or prior conversation unless confirmed.
- Do not reveal confidential client details.
- Avoid generic lines like "I was impressed by your company."

## Output

```markdown
## Hiring Manager Outreach

Hi [Name],

[Message]

Best,
Javon

## Shorter Version
[50-75 words]

## Notes
- Evidence used:
- Missing info:
```
