# Achievement Story Builder

Turn achievements into credible interview stories.

## Inputs

- Achievement, project, bullet, or experience
- Target role or competency
- Known result or outcome
- Confidentiality constraints

## Rules

- Do not invent metrics.
- If results are missing, use non-metric impact or ask for the result.
- Anonymize clients unless Javon approves names.
- Keep stories practical and interview-ready.

## Output

```markdown
## STAR Story: [Theme]

- Situation:
- Task:
- Action:
- Result:
- Best for questions about:

## Short Version
[60-90 second answer]

## Evidence Notes
- Confirmed:
- Needs detail:
```
