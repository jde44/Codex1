# 30-60-90 Day Plan Builder

Build practical plans for final interviews and onboarding.

## Inputs

- Role/company
- Job description
- Known business priorities
- Interviewer concerns or success criteria
- Level: manager, senior manager, director, associate director

## Rules

- Make the plan operational and credible.
- Avoid pretending to know internal facts not provided.
- Use discovery-first language.
- Show leadership maturity without overpromising.

## Output

```markdown
## 30-60-90 Day Plan

### First 30 Days: Learn, Listen, Baseline
- [Action]

### Days 31-60: Align, Prioritize, Mobilize
- [Action]

### Days 61-90: Execute, Measure, Improve
- [Action]

## Success Metrics
- [Metric or observable outcome]

## Executive Talking Points
- [Point]

## Assumptions
- [Assumption]
```
