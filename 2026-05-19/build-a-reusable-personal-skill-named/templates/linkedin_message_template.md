# LinkedIn / Recruiter Message Template

## Short Recruiter Message

```markdown
Hi [Name], I saw the [Role Title] opening at [Company] and wanted to reach out. My background includes 10+ years across cyber, risk, regulatory remediation, banking, controls, and financial services transformation, currently as a Cyber, Risk & Regulatory Advisory Manager at PwC.

The role caught my attention because of its focus on [job-specific theme 1], [theme 2], and [theme 3]. I would be glad to connect if my background looks aligned.

Best,
Javon
```

## 400-Character Why-Fit Response

```markdown
I bring 10+ years across cyber, risk, regulatory remediation, banking, controls, and financial services transformation. My experience supporting issue management, audit readiness, governance, and cross-functional stakeholders aligns well with this role's focus on [priority 1], [priority 2], and [priority 3].
```

## Rules

- Keep it plainspoken and specific.
- Do not oversell.
- Mention PwC only as current employer, not client names.
- Keep the 400-character response under 400 characters, including spaces.
