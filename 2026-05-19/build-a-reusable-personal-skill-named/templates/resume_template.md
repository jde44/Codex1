## Bullet Rules

- Start with strong verbs but avoid exaggerated language.
- Tie each bullet to a job requirement.
- Use metrics only when provided or verified.
- Keep bullets to 1-2 lines where possible.
- Use anonymized client language by default.
- Do not claim hands-on technical implementation unless evidenced.

## Strategy Note

If titles, dates, education, phone, email, or LinkedIn are missing, leave bracketed placeholders rather than inventing.
