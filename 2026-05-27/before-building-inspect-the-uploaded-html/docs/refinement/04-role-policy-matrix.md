# Role And Policy Matrix

## Minimum Role Set For V1

| Role | Why It Matters | Decision Rights | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Executive Sponsor | Ensures remediation and governance work has authority. | Accept business risk and fund remediation. | Sponsor approval, risk acceptance, roadmap. |
| Business Owner | Owns business value and use case priority. | Submit use case, accept business constraints. | Intake approval, business justification. |
| Process Owner | Owns the process where AI is embedded. | Approve workflow changes and control placement. | SOP update, process control map. |
| Data Owner | Owns fitness of data for AI use. | Approve data use and residual data risk. | Data owner attestation, data product contract. |
| Data Owner Lead | Coordinates federated ownership across domains. | Escalate inconsistent ownership and readiness gaps. | Owner forum decision log, remediation tracker. |
| Data Steward | Maintains definitions, CDEs, quality, and metadata. | Certify data definitions and quality issue status. | CDE inventory, glossary, DQ rules, defect log. |
| Report Owner | Owns report integrity and reporting use constraints. | Approve AI-assisted report output use. | Report sign-off, report inventory link. |
| Report Consumer | Confirms output is decision-useful and not overstated. | Accept or reject AI-assisted output for decision support. | Consumer attestation, exception notes. |
| AI Governance Reviewer | Ensures AI policy becomes operating controls. | Approve controlled pilot or monitored operation. | Control checklist, approval memo. |
| Model Risk Partner | Reviews model route, evals, limitations, and frontier use. | Approve, condition, or block model use. | Model route decision, eval pack. |
| Internal Audit | Independently tests policy-to-control evidence. | Raise findings and validate closure. | Audit test script, sampled evidence. |
| Policy Owner | Maintains policy obligations and traceability. | Approve policy changes and retire outdated obligations. | Obligation register, policy-to-control matrix. |

## Enterprise Policies To Embed

| Policy | What Must Be Embedded |
| --- | --- |
| Enterprise AI Governance Policy | Risk tiering, approval gates, evidence expectations, monitoring triggers. |
| Enterprise Data Governance Policy | CDE readiness, lineage, metadata, data product contracts, owner attestations. |
| Data Owner and Stewardship Policy | Data owner, lead, steward, sponsor, report owner, process owner, and consumer responsibilities. |
| Report Governance Policy | AI-assisted commentary controls, source citations, report owner approval, consumer review. |
| Business Process Ownership Policy | AI workflow gates, human approvals, exception handling, and monitoring inside SOPs. |
| Model Risk Management Policy | Model routing, evals, limitations, frontier-use justification, human review. |
| Access Management and Data Protection Policy | Sensitive data masking, retrieval boundaries, memory exclusions, model endpoint restrictions. |
| SDLC and Change Management Policy | Prompt, agent, skill, eval, and monitoring change controls. |
| Third-Party Risk Management Policy | AI provider assessment, contract clauses, retention, processor evidence. |
| Records Retention and Audit Evidence Policy | Retention rules for prompts, outputs, approvals, evals, token logs, memory decisions, memos. |
| Internal Audit Methodology | Policy-to-control test procedures and evidence sampling. |

## Refinement Warning

This matrix is useful, but it can become overwhelming. In the public demo, show the minimum role set and allow drill-down only when the user asks for policy detail.
