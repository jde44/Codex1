# Grill Questions For Product Refinement

Use these questions to sharpen the ARGBriX before go-live. The purpose is to reduce noise, clarify buyer value, and decide what belongs in the first public demo.

## 1. Buyer And Audience

1. Who is the first buyer: Chief AI Officer, Chief Data Officer, CIO/CTO, CRO, Compliance, Internal Audit, or consulting client?
2. Who signs the budget, and what budget does this replace or unlock?
3. Is the first motion a SaaS product, consulting accelerator, internal portfolio demo, or thought leadership asset?
4. What does the buyer already have today: GRC platform, model inventory, data catalog, workflow tool, spreadsheets, or committee decks?
5. What would make a buyer say, “This is credible enough to show my team” in the first 5 minutes?

## 2. Core Pain

1. What is the painful workflow today?
2. Is the real pain policy interpretation, evidence collection, model routing, data readiness, cost visibility, audit prep, or cross-functional accountability?
3. Which manual artifact does the app replace first: intake form, risk assessment, control matrix, model decision, governance memo, audit evidence pack, or committee deck?
4. What is the trigger event: new AI use case, failed audit, regulator inquiry, board concern, model risk review, cost spike, or data quality issue?
5. What happens if the company does nothing?

## 3. Killer Demo Workflow

1. Is regulatory reporting variance commentary still the single killer workflow?
2. What exact decision should the demo help the user make?
3. What is the final “aha” moment: readiness score, blocked approval, memo export, policy-to-control traceability, model cost savings, or audit evidence package?
4. Should the demo be read-only, guided, or allow visitors to submit a small intake?
5. Which screens can be removed from the demo without weakening the story?

## 4. Object Model Sanity Check

1. Which objects are essential for v1?
2. Which objects are impressive but not necessary?
3. Are “memory” and “skills” first-class governance modules for launch, or advanced tabs for later?
4. Is the agent model operationally real, or mainly a way to explain workflow automation?
5. Should “Data Governance Agent” become a primary differentiator or remain a supporting assessment?

## 5. Policy To Action

1. Which enterprise policies must this app embed into operations first?
2. Who owns each policy-to-control mapping?
3. What evidence proves a policy became auditable action?
4. What is the minimum role model: sponsor, data owner, process owner, report owner, reviewer, auditor?
5. How much role complexity is useful, and when does it become consulting wallpaper?

## 6. Data Governance And AI Readiness

1. What does “fit for AI” mean in this product?
2. Is the data readiness score advisory, approval-gating, or audit evidence?
3. Does the app assess data products, reports, processes, or individual AI use cases?
4. How should federated data ownership appear without overwhelming the buyer?
5. Which gaps should block launch: missing owner, missing CDE certification, incomplete lineage, sensitive data controls, unresolved DQ defects, missing report owner sign-off?

## 7. Go-Live Readiness

1. What must be real before sharing publicly: auth, persistence, export, deployment, screenshots, or just a polished demo?
2. What can remain mocked if clearly labeled?
3. What cannot be fake because it damages credibility?
4. What are the 3-5 live demo paths you want to be bulletproof?
5. What should the LinkedIn CTA be: request access, book a walkthrough, read methodology, or view demo?

## 8. Platform Companion Positioning

1. Are we positioning as a companion to Snowflake, Databricks, AWS, Azure, and enterprise data governance tools, or as a standalone governance destination?
2. What do those platforms already do well that we should not pretend to replace: storage, compute, catalog, lineage, BI, ML registry, policy enforcement, or data sharing?
3. What is our “last mile” value after data platforms generate insights: approval workflow, policy-to-control mapping, evidence packaging, model route decision, cost escalation, or audit trail?
4. Which cloud signal should trigger our workflow first: data quality exception, lineage gap, sensitive data usage, model output, report anomaly, query spend, or AI use case intake?
5. Do we read only metadata and governance signals, or do we ever copy business data? If we copy data, what is the control story?
6. Should the first integration story be Snowflake, Databricks Unity Catalog, Microsoft Purview/Fabric, AWS Glue/Lake Formation, or a cloud-neutral metadata adapter?
7. What does “secure data environment in the cloud” mean for the buyer: private networking, customer cloud deployment, no data egress, tenant isolation, encryption, or auditability?
8. Is the strongest message “governed data to governed AI decisions” or “real-time insights to auditable action”?
9. How do we avoid being perceived as a thin dashboard on top of Snowflake/Databricks?
10. What proof would convince a data platform partner that this app expands their ecosystem rather than competing with it?
